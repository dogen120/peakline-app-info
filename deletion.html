Data deletion

Last updated: 13 November 2026

You can request deletion of information associated with Peakline, Meta App ID 1004265152725501, by contacting Immersive Peaks LLC at oded@immersivepeaks.com.

Request deletion

  1. Email with the subject Peakline data deletion.
  2. Identify the business or account involved and describe the information you want deleted. Include only the minimum information needed to locate the records.
  3. We may ask for proportionate evidence that you are the relevant person or an authorized administrator. Do not send a password, access token, authentication code or identity document unless a secure and necessary verification method has first been agreed.

We will assess the request, delete or de-identify information we control where applicable, and explain any information retained for a legal obligation or another valid exception. We will respond within the period required by applicable law. Hosting, security logs and provider backups may be subject to separate retention by their providers.

Stop future Meta access

If you granted Peakline access through a personal Facebook connection, use Facebook’s settings for business integrations or apps and websites to find and remove Peakline. If access was assigned through a business portfolio or system user, ask a portfolio administrator to remove Peakline’s assigned access and revoke its token using Meta Business Settings.

Removing access and deleting existing records are separate actions. Revocation prevents subsequent API access within the revoked grant; it does not automatically erase reports or support records already retained outside Meta. Email us to request that deletion as well. Do not remove an unrelated integration with a similar name.

This information website has no user accounts or deletion form. The email route above is the supported request channel. See the privacy policy for more information.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains how Immersive Peaks LLC, based in the United States, handles information in connection with Peakline (Meta App ID 1004265152725501) and this information website. Contact: oded@immersivepeaks.com.

What Peakline is for

Peakline is an internal integration with the Meta Marketing API for our own businesses. Its initial scope is authorized account checks and advertising performance reporting. It is not a service for connecting external customers’ businesses. This public website provides information and policies; it does not provide a Facebook sign-in, account dashboard or place to enter credentials.

Information involved

When an authorized internal operator enables access, the integration may receive information permitted by Meta and the relevant business or account administrator. For the reporting scope, this can include operator or app identifiers, permission and account-access information, ad-account identifiers and settings, campaign/ad-set/ad metadata, and aggregate advertising performance such as impressions, clicks, spend and conversions. We do not request lead records, customer lists, private messages or payment-card details for this scope.

If you contact us, we receive your contact details and the information you choose to include. Please do not send passwords, access tokens, payment details or unnecessary personal information.

This website’s hosting and delivery services may process technical information such as IP address, request time, browser information and requested pages to deliver and protect the site. The site’s own pages contain no advertising pixels, analytics scripts, forms or application cookies. Hosting-platform processing is described separately below.

Why information is used

We use information to check authorized access, retrieve and understand our own advertising reports, diagnose integration errors, maintain security, answer support or deletion requests, and meet applicable legal obligations. Access to Meta data is limited by the permission and asset selections that Meta actually grants. A public website or published app status does not grant access to someone’s business data.

Service providers and disclosure

Meta provides the platform and APIs and processes information under its own policies. GitHub Pages hosts this public information website. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. The public site is not used to receive or store Meta access tokens or private advertising reports.

OpenAI tools are also used to assist development and diagnostics. App configuration information and selected diagnostic evidence may be processed in those tools; credentials must not be included. The specific data involved depends on the authorized support task. This is distinct from sending all advertising data to a website or an AI service.

Information may also be disclosed when legally required or necessary to protect rights and security. Our authorized use of Meta Platform Data is for the internal purposes described here, not sale or an external data-broker service. Provider practices are described in the Meta Privacy Policy, OpenAI Privacy Policy, and GitHub Privacy Statement.

Storage, retention and security

Internal operational records are kept separately from this public website. Retention depends on the purpose of the record, operational need, applicable legal obligations and any deletion request. Temporary test access should be removed when the test is complete. Data is deleted or de-identified when no longer needed, subject to records that must be retained by law. Service providers may retain their own security, backup or legal records under their policies; we do not promise immediate deletion from systems we do not control.

Only authorized operators should handle account data and credentials. We use the access controls supplied by Meta and our operating environment and avoid including credentials in public pages or diagnostic records. No internet service or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

International processing

Our operator is based in the United States. Hosting, support and service-provider processing may occur in the United States and other countries. We do not represent that processing is confined to one country. Contact us for information about the safeguards and arrangements applicable to your request.

Your choices and requests

You can ask us to access, correct or delete information associated with Peakline by emailing oded@immersivepeaks.com. Depending on your location and circumstances, you may also have rights to object to or restrict processing, receive a portable copy, withdraw consent, or complain to your data-protection authority. We will verify your request proportionately and respond as required by applicable law. If European or UK data-protection law applies, our basis for internal administration, security and responding to requests is our legitimate business interest, except where consent or a legal obligation is the appropriate basis.

To stop future access, an authorized administrator can remove Peakline’s permissions or asset access in Meta. Revoking Meta access does not itself delete existing records held outside Meta. See the data deletion instructions for both steps.

Children and changes

Peakline is intended for authorized adult business operators, not children. Contact us if you believe a child’s information has been provided. We will update this page when the scope or handling of information changes and show the new revision date.
